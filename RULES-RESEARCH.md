## Critical version disagreements

The French PDF and older English text must not be blended silently. Rulespal reports August2026 changes including arrival-together no longer triggering stop-on powers between those arriving together. The French PDF says the opposite. A single backward-passing interpretation is reported by a player, not independently verified against new PDF.

Current FAQ reports publisher correction: Sisyphus on6 warps to start and loses1; **no additional main movement**. Old English explanatory note was erroneous. French agrees with correction.

For Genius predicting1, October2026 publisher ruling reportedly gives next turn to Skipper, overriding Genius. French older note gives Genius another turn first. Clarify chosen version.

MOUTH: newer wording reportedly ends race immediately when only one racer remains, awarding next available place. This avoids infinite farming by Lovable Loser.

## Original Mild Mile artwork
Updated 1 November 2026: Mild Mile uses the original English artwork from the CMYK rulebook mirrored at https://tesera.ru/images/items/2587383/How_to_play_Magical_Athlete_compressed.pdf. The illustration spans PDF pages 10 and 11 (zero-based 9 and 10): embedded images X10 (934×487) and X62 (665×487) are consecutive horizontal portions of the same board. They are extracted, encoded as lossless WebP and placed side by side in SVG without repainting. A 1px overlap prevents browser antialiasing from drawing a seam between the two images. Labels now read Mild Mile / Start. Racetrack coordinates and zoom following are calibrated to this artwork. The previous French asset remains archived in public/boards-original-fr.png; Wild Wilds retains the existing English product image.

The user explicitly chose to preserve the standard Mild / Wild / Mild / Wild schedule. Mild Mile has no special track spaces; athlete powers remain active. The English rulebook's printed page 18 permits choosing a different track schedule before starting a game, but no schedule or rules change is made here.
